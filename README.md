# Quick Jesus Reminder – Privacy Policy

**App Name:** Quick Jesus Reminder
**Developer:** Collette Global LLC
**Platform:** iOS
**Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Quick Jesus Reminder ("QJR", "we", "our", or "us").

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your information when you use the Quick Jesus Reminder mobile application.

Quick Jesus Reminder provides inspirational and educational Christian quotes, daily reminders, notifications, and faith-based content intended to encourage and inspire users.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

Quick Jesus Reminder can be used without creating an account by selecting **"Continue without account."**

When using the app without an account, we do not collect account information such as your first name, email address, or password.

Some features may require an account, including features such as saving favorites, syncing information across devices, and accessing personalized functionality.

If you choose to create an account, we collect the following information:

### Account Information

When you register for an account, we collect:

* First name
* Email address
* Password

Your account information is used to create and maintain your account and provide account-related functionality.

Passwords are handled using appropriate security measures and are not intended to be stored or displayed as plain text.

### User Preferences

We may collect and store preferences you select within the app in order to personalize your experience.

These may include:

* Favorite quotes or content
* Notification preferences
* Preferred notification time
* App settings
* Content preferences

### Push Notification Preferences

If you enable notifications, Quick Jesus Reminder may store information related to your notification preferences, including your selected reminder time.

This information is used to deliver motivational quotes, reminders, and other app notifications according to your preferences.

---

## 3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Quick Jesus Reminder does not intentionally collect:

* Your last name
* Precise geographic location
* Contacts
* Camera recordings
* Microphone recordings
* Photo library content
* Advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising purposes

Quick Jesus Reminder does not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs for behavioral advertising.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through Quick Jesus Reminder to provide and maintain the app and its features.

Your information may be used to:

* Create and maintain your account
* Identify your account when you sign in
* Save your preferences
* Save favorite content
* Sync supported information across devices
* Deliver notifications at your preferred time
* Personalize supported app functionality

## 5. Guest Access

Quick Jesus Reminder allows users to access certain features without creating an account through the **"Continue without account"** option.

When using guest access:

* A first name is not required
* An email address is not required
* A password is not required

Certain features that depend on an account, such as syncing account information or favorites across devices, may not be available in guest mode.

## 6. Subscriptions and Payments

Quick Jesus Reminder offers auto-renewable in-app subscriptions that provide access to premium features and content.

All subscription purchases on iOS are processed through Apple's App Store.

Subscription pricing, available plans, billing periods, and renewal information are displayed within the app before a purchase is completed.

Payment is charged to the user's Apple ID account upon confirmation of purchase.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renewal is canceled in accordance with Apple's subscription terms.

Users can manage or cancel their subscriptions through their Apple ID subscription settings.

Collette Global LLC does not directly collect or process users' Apple payment card information.

Refund requests and payment-related matters are handled according to Apple's applicable App Store policies.
